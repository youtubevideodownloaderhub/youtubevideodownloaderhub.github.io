How to Save YouTube Videos to Your PC: Storage, Formats, and Tips

Save YouTube videos on Windows and Mac, organise them, and choose formats and quality that fit your disk.

Saving a video to your computer is useful when you want to watch it on a big screen, keep a copy of your own recording, or build a personal archive of openly licensed material. A PC gives you more control than a phone: you choose the folder, the format, and the quality, and you can organise files properly. This guide covers the legitimate ways to save videos on Windows and Mac, how much space different qualities need, and how to keep your collection organised and playable.

The official route on desktop

YouTube Premium members can download eligible videos directly on youtube.com by clicking the Download button under the player. The video is stored in the browser and plays offline while the subscription is active. This is the simplest option and the only one YouTube officially supports on desktop. If you do not have Premium, you can still keep track of videos with playlists and the Watch Later list, which is often all you need when your internet connection is reliable.

Formats and quality

MP4 with H.264 video is the safest format: it plays in VLC, Windows Media Player, QuickTime, browsers, and most smart TVs. WebM is common on the web but less universal. For quality, 1080p is the best balance for a laptop or desktop monitor, while 720p saves space and still looks good. 4K is only worth it on a 4K monitor with plenty of free disk space. Audio-only files in M4A or MP3 are much smaller and suit lectures and podcasts.

Organising your downloads

Create a folder structure that will still make sense in a year, for example Videos, then a folder per topic or per creator, with files named by date and title. Add the video's original URL to a text file in the same folder so you can find the source later. Back up anything important to an external drive or cloud storage, because a single hard-drive failure can erase an entire collection. VLC media player is a good free choice for playback, since it handles almost every format without extra codecs.

Troubleshooting downloads

If a download fails, check your free disk space first, then your connection, then whether the video is restricted. Browser downloads can be interrupted by sleep mode, so keep the laptop awake during long downloads. If a saved file will not play, try VLC, which tolerates incomplete or unusual files better than most players. If a video is unavailable in your country, no downloader will fix that lawfully; the video is region-locked by the uploader or a rights holder, and the correct response is to look for an officially licensed version.

Back up your video collection

A video collection stored on one drive is one failure away from disappearing, and hard drives do fail, often without warning. The standard advice is the 3-2-1 rule: three copies of anything important, on two different types of storage, with one copy somewhere else. For videos you own or have a licence to keep, that means the original on your computer, a copy on an external drive, and a copy in cloud storage or at another location. External drives are cheap, and a monthly backup takes minutes once it is set up. Cloud storage is useful for the most important files, but uploading large videos takes time, so back up over Wi-Fi and overnight. Test your backups occasionally by opening a file from the backup drive, because a backup you have never tested is only a hope. If a video is freely available on YouTube and you do not own it, you do not need to back it up at all: the link is the backup.

Frequently Asked Questions

What format should I save videos in?

MP4 is the most compatible format and plays on almost every device and player. WebM works well in browsers but less widely elsewhere.

How much space does a video need?

Roughly 1 to 3 GB per hour at 1080p, and under 500 MB per hour at 480p. 4K can exceed 7 GB per hour.

Can I watch saved videos offline on my PC?

Yes, with any media player such as VLC or the built-in Movies app. Premium downloads on the website also work offline in the browser.
