YouTube to MP3 Info

Understand how audio extraction works, what quality to expect, and what is lawful before you convert anything.

How it works

A YouTube video contains separate audio and video streams. A converter downloads the audio stream and repackages it as an MP3. No new quality is created: YouTube audio is typically around 128-160 kbps, so the MP3 will sound the same as the stream, not better.

What is allowed

Converting your own uploads, Creative Commons audio, and public-domain recordings is fine. Converting copyrighted music, audiobooks, or albums without permission infringes copyright in most countries, even for personal listening.

Lawful alternatives

YouTube Music Premium offers offline audio listening and pays the artists. Many podcasters publish downloadable audio on their own sites, and musicians on Bandcamp often sell MP3s directly.

Risks

Free converter sites are often covered in fake download buttons and pop-ups. Never run an installer a converter offers, never allow its notifications, and scan any file before opening it. This page does not convert audio itself: there is no lawful in-browser converter, and any site that claims one-click conversion of any video should be treated with caution.
